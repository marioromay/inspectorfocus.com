Choose a photograph to calculate print resolution.

Resolution is a guide, not a guarantee.

300+ PPIHigh detail for close viewing.
200–299 PPIGood detail for many prints.
150–199 PPILess detail; viewing distance matters.
Below 150 PPILimited fine detail at close range.

PPI means image pixels per printed inch. Sharpness, noise, paper, the printing process and viewing distance also affect the result. Printer DPI is a different measurement. Calculations use the pixels remaining after the selected crop, without upscaling.
