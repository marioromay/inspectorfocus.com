# Design and brand

Approved interface direction, October 10, 2026.

## Visual language

Use a restrained editorial layout: clear sans-serif typography, generous spacing, thin rules, white or off-white surfaces, dark text, and red interface accents. The Phopod name should be large enough to balance the detailed robot illustration. Keep the lettering plain and monochrome.

Keep tool navigation focused on EXIF Data, Photo Stats, Print Size, Crop / Export, and Contact Sheet. Add a logo-only menu option for the comic, with an accessible label. Avoid restoring separate Privacy, Crop, Resize, or Batch menu items for workflows already combined.

## Logo and motion

Use the approved robot image in its original colors. Dark mode must not invert the logo or turn its white artwork black. The name remains still while the header robot gently floats.

The bottom-right corner mascot also floats and links to the comic. Allow sufficient bottom spacing so it does not cover footer links. Hide the corner mascot while the processing popup is open to avoid duplicating it beside the popup mascot.

The processing popup uses the same floating logo with a subtle shadow. Motion should be gentle rather than distracting. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the interface usable when animation is disabled.

## Comic and footer

The robot’s story lives at /about/ and displays the supplied comic artwork. Clicking the artwork opens the full-size image. Keep meaningful alternative text. Remove the “Read the comic text” section.

The footer link is **Meet Phopod**, not “About Phopod.” Keep **Site by Mario Romay** linked to https://marioromay.com.

## Export branding

Contact-sheet PDF pages carry the robot logo and a site referral inviting recipients to create their own contact sheet at inspectorfocus.com. Thin gray lines separate photographs; filename labels are centered. Branding should remain compact enough to leave photographs as the main content.

## Accessibility

Maintain visible keyboard focus, meaningful control labels, readable contrast in both themes, and accessible status messages. Logo-only links need explicit accessible names. Processing dialogs should announce their status and restore focus on close. Do not rely on red or motion alone to communicate progress or errors.
