Extract the entire ZIP, then open index.htm in a modern desktop browser. Keep its images and assets folders together. Local HTML opening on phones depends on the device and file app.

Your photographs stay on your device. The ZIP contains reduced JPEG review copies; originals stay unchanged. Transparency is flattened to white.
